# Data Sources

The following publicly available sources were reviewed during the Week 1 agribusiness data exploration.

## 1. Open Government Data Platform India

**Website:** https://data.gov.in/

**Purpose:** Provides publicly available government datasets, including agricultural and market-related data.

**Dataset reviewed:** Current Daily Price of Various Commodities from Various Markets (Mandi)

---

## 2. Agmarknet

**Website:** https://agmarknet.gov.in/

**Purpose:** Provides agricultural marketing information, including commodity arrivals and market-level price information across India.

---

## 3. Agricultural Statistics at a Glance

**Source:** Government of India

**Purpose:** Provides agricultural statistics related to crop production, cultivated area, yields, agricultural inputs, and other agricultural indicators.

---

## 4. FAOSTAT

**Website:** https://www.fao.org/faostat/

**Organization:** Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO)

**Purpose:** Provides global statistics related to agriculture, food, crops, livestock, trade, prices, and land use.

---

## 5. World Bank Open Data

**Website:** https://data.worldbank.org/

**Purpose:** Provides economic, social, environmental, and agricultural indicators for countries around the world.

---

## 6. India Data Portal

**Website:** https://indiadataportal.com/

**Purpose:** Provides datasets and visualizations related to agriculture, rural development, markets, and other socio-economic areas in India.

---

## 7. Kaggle

**Website:** https://www.kaggle.com/

**Purpose:** Provides publicly available datasets and data-science resources that can be used for exploratory analysis and practice.
