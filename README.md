# YuvaIntern Task 1 – Agribusiness Data Exploration and Strategic Planning

## Project Overview

This project is part of the YuvaIntern Junior Data Scientist Internship – Week 1 task. The objective is to develop a structured strategy for exploring publicly available agribusiness data and planning a comprehensive data-analysis project.

The project focuses on understanding agricultural market and production patterns, identifying relevant datasets and variables, developing research questions and hypotheses, and defining a systematic methodology for data cleaning, exploratory analysis, statistical analysis, and interpretation.

## Objectives

- Understand the agribusiness domain through publicly available data.
- Identify relevant agricultural datasets and data sources.
- Explore important variables and areas of interest.
- Develop research questions and hypotheses.
- Define data cleaning and preprocessing strategies.
- Plan exploratory, statistical, and comparative analysis.
- Develop a step-by-step analysis roadmap.
- Identify potential data challenges and limitations.

## Data Sources

The research considers publicly available sources including:

- Open Government Data Platform India (data.gov.in)
- Agmarknet
- Agricultural Statistics at a Glance
- FAOSTAT
- World Bank Open Data
- India Data Portal
- Kaggle

## Key Areas of Analysis

The planned analysis focuses on:

- Agricultural commodity price trends
- Market and regional price differences
- Seasonal variations
- Agricultural production patterns
- Relationships between production and market conditions
- Commodity-level price variation

## Research Questions

1. How do agricultural commodity prices vary across different markets and regions?
2. How do commodity prices change over time?
3. Are there noticeable seasonal patterns in agricultural commodity prices?
4. Which commodities show the greatest variation in market prices?
5. How can agricultural production and market-price data be used to identify important agribusiness trends?

## Methodology

The proposed analytical workflow includes:

1. Data source identification
2. Data collection
3. Data quality assessment
4. Data cleaning and preprocessing
5. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
6. Data visualization
7. Statistical analysis
8. Comparative analysis
9. Interpretation and validation
10. Documentation
